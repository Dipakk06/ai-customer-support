# Demo Company Support FAQs

## Return policy
Customers can request a return within 30 days of delivery if the item is unused and in its original packaging. Some final-sale items may not be eligible. The support team must confirm eligibility before promising a refund.

## Refund timeline
After an approved return is received and inspected, refunds are typically processed within 5–7 business days. Bank posting times can vary. The AI assistant must not promise an exact arrival date.

## Shipping and delivery
Standard shipping usually takes 3–5 business days after dispatch. Delivery estimates can change due to carrier delays, weather, or public holidays. Use the order lookup tool for the current demo order status.

## Damaged or defective products
If a product arrives damaged or defective, ask the customer to share the order ID and a short description. Create a support ticket and mark it high priority when damage is reported. Do not promise a replacement or refund until a human reviews the case.

## Payment and billing
If a customer reports a duplicate charge, unexpected payment, or billing dispute, create a high-priority billing ticket. Never ask the customer to share a full card number, CVV, password, or one-time passcode.

## Contact and escalation
If a customer asks for a human representative or the issue cannot be resolved from the available knowledge, create a human escalation ticket. This showcase demo records the ticket locally and does not notify a real support team.

## Privacy
Only request information needed to resolve the issue. This demo uses synthetic data. Do not put real customer personal information into the demo.
